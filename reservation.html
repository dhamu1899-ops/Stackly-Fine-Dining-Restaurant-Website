Frequently Asked Questions

Can you accommodate severe allergies or dietary restrictions?

Yes, absolutely. Our culinary team can tailor both tasting and à la carte dishes for gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, and nut allergies with advance notice.

How far in advance can I book a table?

Online reservations open 60 days in advance at 10:00 AM EST. Private Dining Salon bookings can be made up to 6 months in advance.

Is valet parking available at the venue?

Complimentary valet parking service is provided at our main entrance on Fifth Avenue starting from 4:30 PM daily.

Do you allow children in the dining room?

We welcome guests of all ages; however, we recommend evening dinner service for children ages 8 and above due to the 2.5-hour tasting menu pace.

Need assistance with large parties or corporate bookings? Call Concierge (+1 800 123-4567)
