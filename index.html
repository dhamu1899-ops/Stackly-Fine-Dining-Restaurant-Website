Our Culinary Legacy

A Symphony Of Craftsmanship, Atmosphere & Pure Taste

Founded in 1999 by Executive Chef Alexander Vance, Stackly was born from a singular obsession: to reimagine classical gastronomy through contemporary precision, Salem's finest local herbs, and world-class organic sourcing.

Every dish presented in our main dining hall represents weeks of culinary research—pairing rare Umbrian truffles, A5 Miyazaki Wagyu, and fresh Hokkaido seafood with 2,500 sommelier reserve vintages.
